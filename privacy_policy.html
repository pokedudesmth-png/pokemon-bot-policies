Privacy Policy

For: Pokémon Discord Bot
Effective date: 22 October 2026

Before publishing: Replace “Pokémon Discord Bot” with your bot’s public name and add a contact method you actively monitor.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pokémon Discord Bot (“the Bot”) handles information when people use its commands or when it operates in a Discord server. The Bot is operated by its owner (“we”, “us”).

2. Information the Bot may store or process

Depending on which commands are used, the Bot may process or store:

  • Discord user IDs, and server IDs where server-specific settings are used.
  • Pokémon game progress, such as coins, XP, levels, Pokémon owned, teams, encounters, Pokédex entries, item inventory, and battle-related state.
  • Moderation or configuration records, including bot-ban details and nickname-freeze settings. Nickname-freeze records can include the member ID, server ID, the ID of the person who applied the freeze, and the original and locked nicknames.
  • Information needed to respond to commands, interactions, and server configuration requests.

The Bot’s database is stored by the operator on the machine or hosting service where the Bot runs. The hosting provider may separately process technical data under its own policies.

3. How information is used

Information is used to run game features, preserve game progress, respond to commands, enforce configured moderation features, maintain server settings, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors or service reliability issues.

4. Discord and third-party services

The Bot operates through Discord and may request data that Discord makes available through the permissions and intents configured for the Bot. Discord’s own privacy policy applies to Discord’s services. Pokémon data lookups may use the PokéAPI service; requests to that service are subject to its terms and privacy practices. The hosting provider may also process operational data.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be visible in a Discord server when a command’s normal function displays it, may be processed by Discord or the hosting provider, or may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect the service, or address abuse or security incidents.

6. Retention and deletion

Game and configuration records may remain in the Bot’s local database until they are changed or deleted by the Bot’s functionality or by the operator. Leaving a server or stopping use of the Bot does not necessarily automatically erase stored records. To request review or deletion of information associated with your Discord user ID, contact the operator using the contact method published with this policy. Some records may need to be retained for security, moderation, or operational reasons.

7. Security

The operator should take reasonable steps to protect the Bot’s database, hosting account, and credentials. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure. Never share bot tokens or other secret credentials publicly.

8. Children

The Bot is not designed to collect information directly from children. Users should follow Discord’s minimum age requirements and applicable local laws.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
